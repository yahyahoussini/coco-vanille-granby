# 03 · Competitor landscape and battle map (Granby, La Haute-Yamaska, region)

As of 2026-10-25 (ISO week 2026-W43). Track: competitors, listicles, battle map (Ranking Stack Layers 7.1, 7.5, 13, 14, 15; rank-first `16-market-intelligence.md`).
Machine-readable twin: `data/competitors.csv`. Client facts live in `research/01-business-facts.md` (this file only uses them for comparison).

**Method and limits (read first)**

- About 60 WebSearch queries (standard and extended), run 2026-10-25. WebSearch runs from the **US** and returns **summarized** results. This is **not** a Granby-localized Google SERP, not a map pack and not an AI-engine run. "Who owns a prompt" below means **who shows up in the surfaced sources**, not a measured position.
- Ratings are third-party snapshots (RestoMontreal quoting Google, Three Best Rated, Yelp, Tripadvisor). They conflict often; both values are listed. No Google Maps page could be opened.
- "Inferred" = deduced from URL patterns or snippets, not confirmed on the page. "UNVERIFIED" = surfaced once, not cross-checked. `[[FACT NEEDED: …]]` = only the owner or a manual check can answer.
- Mandatory next step: the Layer 7 baseline from a phone in Granby (map pack + organic top 10) for the 30 prompts. Until then, everything here is a hypothesis map.

## 0. Ten findings that change decisions

1. **The custom-cake niche in Granby has two web-ready owners: Cake Design Granby and Avec un Grand A.** Both run their own shop with a cart and published prices. Cake Design publishes a per-portion price ($5/portion, minimum 16) and pickup slots. Avec un Grand A publishes per-size prices and a 48-hour lead time. ([cakegranby.ca](https://cakegranby.ca/products/gateau-sur-mesure), [cakegranby.com tarifs](https://www.cakegranby.com/saveurs_tarifs.php), [avecungranda.com](https://www.avecungranda.com/commande-en-ligne))
2. **Rose Gâteaux is the only Granby business surfaced that markets wedding cakes as a specialty.** It also claims a service area (Granby, Bromont, Saint-Hyacinthe), offers tastings and lists sample prices. ([rosegateaux.com](https://rosegateaux.com/), [threebestrated cakes](https://threebestrated.ca/cakes-in-granby-qc))
3. **Coco Vanille is in the "bakery" lists but missing from the "cake / pâtisserie" list.** Three Best Rated ranks it #2 of 3 for Granby *bakeries*. The French page titled « 3 Meilleurs Pâtisseries à Granby » lists Cake Design, Rose Gâteaux and Avec un Grand A instead. ([TBR bakeries](https://threebestrated.ca/bakeries-in-granby-qc), [TBR cakes](https://threebestrated.ca/cakes-in-granby-qc), [TBR FR pâtisseries](https://threebestrated.ca/fr-gâteaux-in-granby-qc))
4. **Stale listicles leave open slots.** Pain Pain closed on 2026-06-16 ([La Voix de l'Est](https://www.lavoixdelest.ca/affaires/affaires-locales/2026/06/16/les-proprios-de-la-boulangerie-pain-pain-tournent-la-page-BGIVLUIEIRCHHJX6KZ5J2RWZZQ/), [M105](https://m105.ca/nouvelle/granby-fermetures-a-granby-scores-et-pain-pain-tirent-leur-reverence/), [Granby Express](https://www.granbyexpress.com/actualites/les-fourneaux-de-la-boulangerie-pain-pain-seteignent/)). Yet it is still #1 on Three Best Rated's bakery list and first on Yelp's list "updated October 2026". Mélisse (#3 on Three Best Rated) carries a "may be temporarily closed" flag on RestoMontreal.
5. **No Granby, Bromont or Magog wedding-cake vendor appears on WeddingWire.ca.** Sherbrooke's "10 best" page shows only 2 results. ([WeddingWire Sherbrooke](https://www.weddingwire.ca/wedding-cakes/quebec/sherbrooke)) The regional wedding-cake board is almost empty online. That only helps if Coco Vanille really makes wedding cakes: `[[FACT NEEDED: wedding capacity]]` (01-business-facts.md found no evidence).

([RestoMontreal Granby](https://www.restomontreal.ca/resto/mont-rose-patisserie-cafe-granby/23174/en/))
